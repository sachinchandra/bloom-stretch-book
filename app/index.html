Pregnancy stretches

The stretches and labour positions from your prenatal class, redrawn as moving figures. Tap any card to watch it slowly with the instructor's cues, the technique, and what the partner does.

Before you start
  • Move only within a comfortable range. Mild stretch, never pain.
  • Breathe in through the nose, out through the mouth. Never hold the breath.
  • Stop and call your doctor or midwife for bleeding, fluid leaking, dizziness, chest pain, or regular tightenings before 37 weeks.
  • The labour positions are reminders of what your instructor taught. Check them with your care team first.
Emergency? Call 112 (all emergencies) or 108 (ambulance). Go to the nearest hospital with a paediatric emergency.
