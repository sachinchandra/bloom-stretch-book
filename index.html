Pregnancy · Weeks 1–13

First trimester

  • First antenatal visitRegister by 10–12 weeks: blood group, haemoglobin, thyroid, sugar and infection tests.
  • Folic acid every dayThrough the first trimester, as your doctor prescribes.
  • Dating and NT scansDating scan around 7–9 weeks. NT scan with double-marker test at 11–14 weeks.
  • Ease morning sicknessSmall, frequent meals, ginger and sips of water. Call if you can't keep fluids down.

Second trimester

Pregnancy · Weeks 28–40

Third trimester

  • Count kicks dailyFrom 28 weeks. Note how long 10 movements take.
  • Tdap between 27 and 36 weeksProtects your newborn from whooping cough.
  • Pack the hospital bagBy 34–35 weeks, with documents and baby's going-home set.
  • Sleep on your sideLeft side is best, with a pillow between your knees.

Birth · The big day

Labour and birth

  • Know the signsRegular contractions about 5 minutes apart, waters breaking, or bleeding: time to go.
  • Move and breatheUpright positions, the birth ball, wall lean and partner pressure on the lower back.
  • Have numbers readyYour hospital, your doctor, and 108 for an ambulance.
  • The golden hourSkin-to-skin and the first breastfeed within an hour of birth.

Baby · 0–3 months

Newborn

  • Feed 8–12 times a dayOn cue, day and night. 6+ wet nappies from day 5 means enough milk.
  • Birth, 6, 10 and 14-week vaccinesBCG, OPV, Hep B, then the 6, 10 and 14-week rounds.
  • Watch jaundice and the cordCheck skin colour in daylight. Keep the cord clean and dry.
  • Safe sleepOn the back, on a firm flat cot, nothing loose near the face.
  • Your recoveryRest, eat well, and go for your 6-week postnatal check.

Baby · 3–6 months

Growing

  • Tummy time every dayBuilding up to 45–60 minutes in total by 6 months.
  • Laughs, rolls and reachesHolds the head steady and pushes up on the arms.
  • Flu and typhoid vaccinesThe flu vaccine from 6 months, and typhoid (TCV) between 6 and 9 months.
  • Get ready for solidsWatch for sitting with support and interest in food around 6 months.

Baby · 6–12 months

Exploring

  • MMR at 9 monthsPlus the PCV booster around 12–15 months.
  • Sits, crawls, first wordsBabbles "mama", waves bye-bye, pulls up to stand.
  • Baby-proof the homeCover sockets, lock away medicines, and keep button batteries out of reach.

Toddler · 1–2 years

On the move

  • First steps and wordsWalking alone, pointing, saying a few words, then two-word phrases.
  • BoostersMMR 2, chickenpox, and the DTP, Hib and IPV boosters at 15–18 months.
  • Family mealsThe same food as the family, cut small, with less salt and spice.
  • Read and talk every dayName things, sing, and read picture books together.

How it works

Set up in two minutes.

STEP 1

Sign in

Use Google or your email. Both partners can sign in on their own phones.

STEP 2

Tell us where you are

Add your due date or your baby's birth date. BloomMom picks the right stage, tests, vaccines and daily cards.

STEP 3

Swipe through today

Each morning, your medicines, stretches and baby-care actions arrive as cards. Swipe them done, and everything saves to your account.
