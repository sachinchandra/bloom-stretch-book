Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

BloomMom (bloommom.in) is a free guide to pregnancy stretches and baby care. This policy explains what we store and why.

Without an account

You can use everything without signing in. Your ticks, baby's date of birth, emergency card and stretch log are then saved only in your browser on your device. We don't receive them.

With an account

If you sign in with Google or your email, we store:

  • your email address and, for Google sign-in, your name and profile picture as provided by Google;
  • the progress you choose to save: shopping and vaccine ticks, baby's date of birth, the emergency card details you type, and your stretch log.

We use this only to sync your progress across your devices. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for marketing. Only you can read your saved data; it is protected by per-user access rules.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) in its Mumbai region, and the website is served by Vercel.

Deleting your data

Sign in, open your account menu and choose Delete my saved data. To delete your sign-in account too, email sachin@enigmaticitsolutions.com.

Medical information

BloomMom gives general guidance only and is not medical advice. Always follow your doctor or midwife.
